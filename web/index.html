🔥 How to Create a Bootable USB (3 Easy Steps)

Step 1
Download Recommended Tool

We recommend Ventoy (simplest multi-boot) or Rufus (write in DD image mode). On Linux/macOS, use balenaEtcher.

Step 2
Flash to USB Drive

Select your USB drive (4GB+), choose the downloaded .iso file, and click Flash / Start.

Step 3
Boot & Live Test

Reboot your PC, press F12 / F9 / Esc to enter the Boot Menu, select your USB, and test Aero Linux live with zero risk.
