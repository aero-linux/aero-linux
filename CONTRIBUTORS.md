### Acknowledgments & Upstream Projects

Aero Linux stands on the shoulders of the open-source community:
* **Linux Kernel Team & Canonical/Debian** for the rock-solid base system and live-build toolchains.
* **SwayWM & Waybar Teams** for lightweight Wayland window management and status bar ergonomics.
* **Linux Mint Team** for inspirations across Cinnamon, Nemo, and user-first desktop utilities.
* **Ollama & vLLM Communities** for making local AI inference accessible on developer hardware.
* **Calamares Team** for the flexible graphical OS installer framework.
