Hi Product Hunt! 👋

I'm Ronit Gupta, creator of Aero Linux.

Modern operating systems have become bloated with background telemetry, advertising daemons, and sluggish indexing services that consume 2GB to 4GB of RAM before you even write your first line of code.

As developers running Docker containers and local AI models (DeepSeek-R1, Llama 3), hardware memory is our most valuable asset.

I built Aero Linux to give engineers back their machine:
⚡ 310MB Idle RAM baseline (Wayland + Sway)
🧠 Dynamic zRAM ZSTD compression (effectively turns 16GB RAM into ~25GB+)
🤖 1-Click Local AI runtime with automated NVIDIA CUDA & AMD ROCm setup
🔋 Hardware 80% battery threshold capping to extend laptop lifespan
🪟 Windows-Friendly Mode: familiar bottom taskbar and shortcuts (Win+E, Alt+Tab)
